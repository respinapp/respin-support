# RE:SPIN! Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

RE:SPIN! is designed to play music stored locally on your iPhone.

## Data stored on your device

Your music files, playlists, settings, metadata, and artwork are stored locally on your device.

RE:SPIN! does not require a user account.

## Data collection

RE:SPIN! does not collect personal information for advertising, tracking, analytics, or profiling.

RE:SPIN! does not use advertising identifiers and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Online metadata lookup

If you choose to use the online metadata lookup feature, RE:SPIN! may send text information about a track, such as its title, artist, album, or version information, to third-party metadata services in order to search for matching metadata and artwork.

These services may include:

- MusicBrainz
- Cover Art Archive
- Apple iTunes Search

Your audio files themselves are not uploaded to these services.

Online metadata lookup is optional and is only performed when you choose to use that feature.

## Permissions and device data

RE:SPIN! does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.

The app may access audio files that you explicitly import or make available to the app.

## Data sharing

RE:SPIN! does not sell your data.

RE:SPIN! does not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

When you use online metadata lookup, the track information needed for the search is transmitted to the metadata providers described above.
